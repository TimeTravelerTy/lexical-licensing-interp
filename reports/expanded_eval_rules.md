# Expanded eval set with the stored v: behavior, u_md transfer, cross-fitted LEACE, DAS chain (paper plan, item 13)

Declared Nov 23, 2026, before any run. Pythia-1.4B first, then Gemma-3-1B and OLMo-2-1B with the same rules (model-specific sites, folds and margins as in `reports/replication_criteria.md` and `reports/umd_transfer_rules.md`).

## Eval set

- **Original:** the 126 original pairs, items as stored (passive test: `data/das_round2/passive_test/items.csv`, source `original`; LEACE: the necessity run's 15,750 items).
- **New:** `data/verb_expansion/expanded_pairs.csv` minus the 24 pairs flagged `das_train_overlap` (they share a verb with v's training pairs): **142 pairs** (123 from the Nov 18 batch, 19 from R5-D11). They replace the 19 earlier eval pairs (17 of whose intransitives were re-paired into the expanded set).
- **Contexts (cross design):** every new pair appears in all 126 curated contexts of its analysis (passive test: the 126 `was` prefixes of `passives.jsonl`; LEACE: the 126 `passive_2` `was` contexts). No new context curation.
- **Classes:** the bad (intransitive) verb's round-2 class. Original: `intrans_classes_final.csv`. New: `expanded_intrans_class.csv` (`plain` / `prep_object`).
  - **Primary: plain.** Original plain (59 pairs) and new plain (74). The stored passive "primary" population had 64 because it included 5 plain pairs of the 19 earlier eval pairs.
  - **Prepositional (`prep_object`): a separate group,** always reported apart and never pooled into the primary numbers.
  - Original `contaminated_bad` stays as before (reported with and without).
- **Frequency:** a continuous covariate (intransitive participle Zipf and summed-lemma Zipf), with the band tags (head / near-head / tail / gap / xtail / out) for description only.
- **Robustness rows:** without `selection`-flagged intransitives; without FreqBLiMP-source transitives; single-token pairs only.

Every number is reported three ways, side by side: **original 126, new 142, pooled 268** (each restricted to the primary population, then the prepositional group separately).

## A. Behavior (forward passes only)

- Natural passive *by* gap per pair: log P(" by" | T passive) − log P(" by" | I passive) at the participle, in the curated contexts; the passive-test estimator and bootstrap (pairs within band × contexts).
- The share of pairs with a positive gap, and the gap regressed on the intransitive's participle Zipf (slope with a pair-bootstrap CI).
- Active class behavior ("<Subj> has/have <participle>", M = log P(O) − log P(I)): the share of new verbs on their class's side in ≥ 4 of 7 items.

## B. DAS chain with the stored v (Pythia rule sites 8 and 12, primary 17)

`build_passive_test.py` with `--eval-pairs` = the 142 new pairs (the originals first, in the stored order), so the original rows of the plan are generated by the same draws and must reproduce the stored plan exactly. Donors are v's held-out training pairs, as stored; each new pair's item gets a random fold per split, as the original non-DAS pairs did.

1. **Passive *by* transfer:** `run_passive_test.py` with the stored bases; D_bad(*by*) and D_bad(O), the stored classifier against δ_s, and D / natural gap.
2. **OR and TC** (`build_constructions.py` on the expanded passive items, plain pairs, past forms of the new verbs from `expanded_pairs.csv`): D(O) with the stored classifier.

Filler-gap and in-range are not rerun on the expanded set (their verb gates and in-range targets are per verb and were declared on the 126).

## C. u_md transfer

The same plans with the stored u_md bases (`results/das_round2/umd/bases_umd.npz`, one per (split, fold), at the sites where u was stored: 8, 12, 17): passive D_bad(*by*) and OR / TC D(O), reported as ratios to v on the same draws.

## D. Cross-fitted LEACE (necessity)

`run_leace_necessity.py` on the expanded items: verb-level 5-fold cross-fitting over **all 268 pairs** (the stored fold procedure: pairs shuffled within band × token-pattern strata, seed 0), conditions single_L, scrub 0–12 (primary, as Settled 1), scrub_all, with `--cumulative`. Measure: share of the natural *by* gap remaining, with the stored estimator and bootstrap. Reported for original, new and pooled items (primary; prepositional separately), next to the stored 126-only result (0.59 [0.35, 0.70] at scrub 0–12).

- The 20-permutation control is run on the pooled set if B and D pass their checks (the true-label CI must lie below the permutations' 5th percentile, as in Settled 1).
- In-sample and the learning curve are not rerun now.

## Checks (all must pass, or that analysis is invalid)

- **Plan reproduction:** the original rows of the expanded passive plan equal the stored plan (same row content in the same order); the stored v's D_bad(*by*) on the original 126 recomputed from the new run matches the stored `transfer_summary.csv` within 1e-4 (same bases, same prompts; bf16 batch effects are bounded by the Amendment 2 bar of `das_expanded_stability_rules.md`, so a mismatch above 1e-4 but within that bar is reported, not treated as a failure).
- **Prompts:** every new participle has the same token count in every context; the new verbs' actives are built exactly as the originals'.
- **LEACE:** the original items' natural log P(" by") equals the stored necessity run's within 1e-4 (fp32); the stored fit diagnostics (cross-covariance residual, identity hook, scrub replay) pass as before.
- **Passive test:** self-patch = unpatched; held-out recompute under the Amendment 2 bar.

## Reading

Descriptive, three columns (original / new / pooled), with CIs. Two declared comparisons:

1. **Transfer generalizes to new verbs:** D_bad(*by*) on new plain pairs has a 95% CI above 0 at both rule sites, and the new / original ratio point estimate is ≥ 0.5. Otherwise "transfer weaker on new verbs", with the numbers. Same for u_md.
2. **Verb-general share with twice the verbs:** the pooled cross-fitted scrub 0–12 share remaining, with its CI, against the stored 126-only 0.59. Reported as "lower", "similar" or "higher" by whether the pooled CI lies below 0.59, contains it, or lies above it.

**Which number the paper quotes.** For the verb-general share (the plan's "one number for how shared"), the pooled 268-pair cross-fitted estimate replaces the 126-pair one as the headline if its checks pass, because the CI width was the reason for the expansion; the 126-pair number is shown next to it. For transfer, the 126-pair results stay primary and the new pairs are a generalization test. A prepositional-group result never enters a primary number.

## Gemma and OLMo

After Pythia, the same steps (A–D) at each model's rule sites (Gemma 5 / 8, primary 15; OLMo 9 / 11, primary 13) with its stored bases, u_md bases, folds and δ. Each model's own behavior filter is not applied to eval items (eval pairs are never filtered; the natural gap and its sign are reported).

## Amendment (Nov 23, before any run; after Codex review)

1. **Counts.** Original / new / pooled: plain 59 / 74 / 133; prepositional 60 / 68 / 128; contaminated 7 / 0 / 7; all 126 / 142 / 268. The passive reproduction check compares v on the original plain pairs with the stored `primary, original pairs only` row (not `primary (plain)`), with its own tolerance: max |ΔD_bad(*by*)| ≤ 0.02 nats over the sites (bf16 batching differs because the prompt set differs). A larger difference stops the reading until explained.
2. **LEACE comparisons, in this order.**
   - **(a) Same items, different fitting:** the stored 126-pair run against the original-126 subset of the expanded run, on identical items and one set of bootstrap draws (paired difference of the share remaining), for all classes (the stored definition of 0.59) and plain only. This isolates the change in the fitted erasers (2× training verbs, new folds: 95 of 126 original pairs change fold).
   - **(b)** Original / new / pooled under the expanded fitting, all classes and plain / prepositional.
   - The pooled number is called *lower* / *higher* if its CI lies below / above the stored point estimate 0.59, else *compatible with the stored point estimate* (not "similar": the stored estimate's own uncertainty is not propagated).
   - **Headline:** if checks pass, the pooled all-class cross-fitted share (erasers fit on all 268 pairs, all classes, as the stored run fit on all 126) replaces 0.59 as the headline, reported with its natural and erased gaps, with (a) next to it and the plain-only pooled number (133 pairs). Erasers are fit on all classes; restricting the reported average to plain verbs is a separate choice and both are shown.
3. **Permutation control:** not run on the expanded set now. The stored control is in-sample and would not calibrate a cross-fitted headline; an expanded cross-fitted permutation control is optional if time allows.
4. **Transfer reading, reworded.** New / original on one set of context and donor draws: the ratio D_new / D_orig and the contrast D_new − 0.5·D_orig, each with a CI. *Generalizes:* D_new's CI above 0 and the contrast's CI above 0. *Generalization criterion not met:* otherwise, split into *weaker* (contrast CI below 0) and *inconclusive*. Site 17 (v's *by* is negative there) is descriptive.
5. **Context support (descriptive caveat).** Original passive pairs have 122–125 contexts (contexts sharing the pair's own prefix text are removed), new pairs all 126 context IDs (109 distinct prefixes); in LEACE originals have 125 and new pairs 126. The claim is generalization to new verbs in these curated contexts, which were curated for the original transitives.
6. **Bands:** frequency stays a continuous covariate (slope on intransitive Zipf); band rows are not used for new pairs (their tags include near-head, gap and out).
7. **Gemma / OLMo:** the single-token robustness row uses each model's own token counts.
